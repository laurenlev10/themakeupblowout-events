Quick help

  • Normal day: leave it on Event schedule. It starts the energetic opening and the announcements on its own.
  • Speaker offline? Open the Soundtrack app on the speaker tablet, keep the screen on and plugged in, Wi-Fi connected.
  • Music stopped? Tap Play. Still silent → switch to Main playlist.
  • Never pick the short Opening list in the Soundtrack app — by hand it repeats the same 4 songs.
  • A song not right for the room? Tap Next song.
