This policy covers the phone numbers that receive text messages from our toll-free number (866) 551-0755, operated by The Makeup Blowout Sale Group Inc. ("we", "us"). It applies alongside our SMS Terms & Conditions.

What we collect

The only recipients of this number are the two owners of the company. We hold their mobile numbers, which they entered themselves, and the record of the messages sent to them. We collect no phone numbers from customers or the public through this number.

The numbers are used solely to deliver internal operational alerts about our own business: event schedule changes, task notices, dashboard links and system alerts. They are never used for marketing.

No sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Numbers are processed only by our SMS provider to deliver the messages.

Storage & security

Numbers and provider credentials are stored as encrypted secrets, accessible only to our internal automation and the company owners.

Opting out
