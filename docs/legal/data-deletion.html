Data Deletion Instructions

The Makeup Blowout Sale Group Inc. respects your privacy. If you have previously signed up for our SMS list, our event reminders, or interacted with our Meta-connected services (Facebook Page, Instagram), you have the right to request that we delete the personal data we hold about you.

What data we may hold

  • Phone number — only if you signed up for our SMS event-reminder list.
  • Email address — only if you provided one when registering for an event.
  • First name — only if you provided one when registering.
  • Public profile information (name, profile picture) returned by Meta when you message our Facebook Page or Instagram account.

We do not store payment information. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

How to request deletion

Send an email to the address below with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. Include the phone number, email address, or Instagram/Facebook handle you used so we can identify your record:

info@makeupblowoutsalegroup.com

We will confirm receipt of your request within 7 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

SMS-only opt-out (faster)

If you only want to stop receiving SMS messages, simply reply STOP to any message we send. Your number will be removed from our list immediately. To also delete the historical record, follow the email steps above.

Questions

For any questions about this process or our data practices, contact info@makeupblowoutsalegroup.com.
