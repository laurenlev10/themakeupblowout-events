What is the Makeup Blowout Sale?

A travelling weekend pop-up where prestige makeup, skincare, haircare and fragrance brands are sold at up to 75% off retail. It runs Friday to Sunday, 10AM–5PM, usually in a hotel ballroom or event hall, and has been touring the US for ten years.

Is there an entry fee or a ticket?

No. Entry and parking are free and no ticket is needed. Signing up by text before you come gets you a free eyeshadow at the door.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Come early on Friday for the widest selection.
